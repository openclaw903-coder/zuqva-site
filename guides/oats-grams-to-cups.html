How many grams in a cup of oats?

A US cup of dry rolled oats is about 81 grams, spooned in and levelled. That figure comes from USDA FoodData Central. Most kitchen charts print 80 to 90 grams for the same cup. Steel-cut oats are a different story: a cup weighs about 160 grams, close to double, because the grains are cut, not flattened. A cup of cooked oatmeal weighs about 234 grams.

How many grams in a cup of oats by type?

Every figure is for one US cup of dry oats, spooned in and levelled, unless it says cooked.

Cooked oatmeal, from steel-cut oatsabout 242

Rolled oats are the ones most recipes mean when they say a cup of oats. Quick oats are rolled oats cut smaller, so they pack a little tighter, at 81 to 85 grams, and instant oats land near 80. Steel-cut oats are the outlier: the whole groat is chopped into pieces, so there is almost no air between them, and a cup weighs about 160 grams. Swap steel-cut in by the cup and the weight doubles, but steel-cut takes far longer to cook.

Why do sources say 80 or 90?

They measured different oats, and they measured them differently. The USDA lists 81 grams for a cup of uncooked old-fashioned oats. King Arthur Baking lists 89 grams on its ingredient weight chart. Quaker puts 40 grams on the label for a half cup serving, which works out to 80 grams a cup. Bob's Red Mill lists 98 grams, because its flakes are thicker and denser. Oat flakes are flat and springy, so they bridge across each other and trap air in the cup. How thick the flakes are, and how the cup was filled, moves the number by 10 grams or more, and scooping straight from the bag can add 10 to 15 percent. Spooned in and levelled, most cups land between 80 and 90 grams.

Dry oats vs cooked oats

Oats drink up water as they cook: one cup dry comes out of the pot weighing close to 470 grams. A cup of dry oats and a cup of cooked oatmeal are two different amounts of food, even though both are called a cup.

Oats1 cup dryCooked yield1 cup cooked
Rolled81 gabout 2 cups234 g
Steel-cut160 gabout 2 cups242 g

Half a cup of dry rolled oats, about 40 grams, cooks into about one cup of oatmeal, about 234 grams. One cup dry, 81 grams, cooks into about two cups, close to 470 grams. The calories stay with the dry oats, not the water: one cup of dry rolled oats carries about 307 calories, with 10.6 grams of protein, 54.8 grams of carbs, 5.3 grams of fat, and 8.2 grams of fibre. A cup of cooked oatmeal carries about 158 calories, with 5.9 grams of protein, 27.1 grams of carbs, 3.1 grams of fat, and 4.0 grams of fibre. The water adds weight, not energy.

What about a metric cup, and tablespoons?

A metric cup holds 250 millilitres, not 236.6, so it holds about 6 percent more: a metric cup of dry rolled oats is about 85 to 95 grams. A tablespoon of dry rolled oats is about 5 grams, since 16 tablespoons fill a cup. Half a cup is 40 to 45 grams, and a quarter cup is 20 to 23 grams.

Worked example

Porridge for four. The recipe calls for 2 cups of dry rolled oats. At 81 grams a cup, that is 162 grams, and at the 90 gram end of the range it is 180 grams. Cook it with 4 cups of water or milk. It makes about 4 cups of porridge, around 940 grams, and at about 158 calories a cooked cup, the pot holds about 630 calories, or about 158 calories a bowl before toppings. A recipe that lists 100 grams of dry oats needs about one and a quarter cups: 100 divided by 81 is 1.2. One cup of dry oats makes about two servings as a breakfast, about 40 grams dry each.

How to measure a cup of oats

Spoon the oats into a dry measuring cup and level the top with the flat edge of a knife. Do not dip the cup into the bag: scooping packs the flakes and can add 10 to 20 grams, which is a quarter of a cupful on a small recipe. If a recipe gives weights in grams, weigh the oats and skip the cup. A kitchen scale removes the flake thickness from the measurement entirely.

In a Pakistani kitchen

In Pakistan oats are sold as Quaker oats in supermarkets, and a cup weighs the same 81 grams as anywhere else. Oats are not dalia, which is cracked wheat, so a recipe that calls for oats does not work with dalia by the cup.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of dry oats?

About 81 grams for rolled oats in a US cup, according to the USDA. Most charts print 80 to 90 grams, because flake thickness and packing move the number. Steel-cut oats are about 160 grams a cup.

How much cooked oatmeal does 1 cup of dry oats make?

About two cups, close to 470 grams, for rolled oats. Half a cup dry, about 40 grams, makes about one cup cooked, about 234 grams.

Are steel-cut oats heavier than rolled oats?

Yes, about twice as heavy by the cup. A cup of dry steel-cut oats is about 160 grams, while a cup of dry rolled oats is about 81 grams. Measure steel-cut oats by weight when a recipe was written for rolled oats.
