How many grams in a cup of shredded cheese?

One level US cup of loosely packed shredded cheddar weighs 113 grams and carries about 455 calories, from USDA FoodData Central. But that number only holds for cheese shredded fresh from a block. The same cup filled from a bag of pre-shredded mozzarella weighs 86 grams. A cup of shredded cheese is mostly air, so the cheese, the cut and the packing all change the weight.

Shredded cheddar by the cup, spoon and fraction

Every figure is for one level US cup, 236.6 millilitres, of loosely packed shredded cheddar, from USDA FoodData Central. Cheddar runs 403 calories per 100 grams, so the cup works out to about 455 calories. Converter charts list the same cup at 444 to 452 calories, which is the same figure with a little rounding.

One cup holds 16 tablespoons, and 100 grams is just under a cup, about 0.88 of one. A standard 8-ounce bag holds 227 grams, about 2 cups. A 4-ounce block, 113 grams, shreds into about 1 cup, which is the rule cheese makers print on their own sites.

Why one cup can weigh 86 grams or 113 grams

Mozzarella shows the gap best, because USDA measures it twice. Whole-milk mozzarella, shredded, is listed at 112 grams a cup and 335 calories (FDC 170845). Low-moisture part-skim mozzarella, the kind sold pre-shredded in bags, is a separate entry at 86 grams a cup and about 261 calories, at 304 calories per 100 grams.

The difference is air and coating, not a mistake. Bagged shreds are cut fine, tossed in a little potato starch or cellulose so they do not clump, and they sit loose in the bag. They trap more air and pack less tightly, so a cup holds less cheese. Cheese shredded fresh from a block on a box grater falls in longer, heavier shreds that settle closer together.

The same thing happens with parmesan. Finely grated parmesan from a canister weighs 100 grams a cup. Grated on a microplane at home, it comes out so fluffy that a cup holds only about 57 grams. Two cups can hold the same cheese at half the weight, which is why recipes that give grams are worth following over recipes that give cups.

The cheese ladder

Cheese does not follow one conversion. A cup of a soft, wet cheese weighs more than twice a cup of a dry, grated one, because the form decides how much air is in the cup. All figures are USDA measured cup weights.

Cream cheese is the odd one out. It has no air in it at all, so a standard 8-ounce brick is exactly 1 cup, 232 grams. It is the only cheese where 8 ounces equals 1 cup. Feta is the other surprise: USDA measures crumbled feta at 150 grams a cup, well above the 114 grams some charts imply, because the crumbles pack down harder than shreds do.

Worked example: pizza and mac and cheese

A 12-inch pizza usually takes about 1 and a half cups of shredded whole-milk mozzarella. That is 168 grams and about 502 calories of cheese on the whole pizza, or about 63 calories a slice if it is cut into eight. Using bagged part-skim mozzarella instead, 1 and a half cups weighs only 129 grams, so the pizza gets less cheese than the recipe intended even though the cups match.

For mac and cheese, 2 cups of shredded cheddar is 226 grams and about 910 calories. That is exactly one 8-ounce bag, or two 4-ounce blocks. If a recipe calls for 2 cups and you weigh 226 grams, you have the right amount whichever way you measured it.

Logging one form as another

A cup of shredded cheddar carries about 455 calories, 28 grams of protein and 37 grams of fat. Two tablespoons sprinkled over a baked potato weigh 14 grams and add about 57 calories, which is small on its own and easy to forget when it happens three times a day.

It is the same mistake as with dry and cooked grains: the entry has to match the form you weighed. Log a cup of bagged mozzarella as fresh-shredded cheddar and you count about 455 calories instead of 261, an overcount of nearly 200 calories. Log it the other way round and you miss the same amount. US cup, metric cup and UK cup

A US cup holds 236.6 millilitres, so 113 grams of shredded cheddar. A metric cup, used in Australia, New Zealand and Canada, holds 250 millilitres, about 6 percent more, so about 119 grams of cheddar, 118 grams of whole-milk mozzarella, 106 grams of grated parmesan and 159 grams of crumbled feta. A UK cup is also 250 millilitres in modern recipes, so use the metric figures there too. When a recipe gives grams, use the grams and skip the cup entirely.

In a Pakistani kitchen

Shredded cheese turns up most often on pizza, in burgers and sandwiches, and melted over fries. Mozzarella is the usual pick for pizza because it stretches, while cheddar gives a sharper taste in a sandwich or on loaded fries. Paneer is not a substitute in these conversions: it is pressed fresh, crumbles differently, and a cup of crumbled paneer does not weigh the same as a cup of shredded cheddar, so weigh it on its own if a recipe needs grams.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of shredded cheese?

113 grams for a loosely filled cup of shredded cheddar, about 455 calories, from USDA FoodData Central. The same cup of bagged pre-shredded part-skim mozzarella weighs only 86 grams.

Why does bagged mozzarella weigh only 86 grams a cup?

USDA lists bagged pre-shredded part-skim mozzarella as its own food at 86 grams a cup. It is fluffier than cheese shredded fresh from a block, and the anti-caking starch keeps the shreds apart, so more air fits in the cup.

How many grams is 1 cup of grated parmesan?

100 grams for finely grated parmesan, from USDA FoodData Central. A microplane grate comes out fluffier and lighter, about 57 grams a cup.

How many cups is 8 oz of shredded cheese?

About 2 cups. 8 ounces is 227 grams, and one cup of shredded cheddar weighs 113 grams, so a standard 8-ounce bag holds about 2 cups.

Every figure on this page is approximate, because brands, moisture and grate size vary a little, and how a cup is filled changes the weight. For exact work, weigh the cheese.
