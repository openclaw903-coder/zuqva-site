How many grams in a cup of milk?

One level US cup of whole milk weighs 244 grams. That is the USDA FoodData Central figure for whole milk, 3.25 percent milkfat (FDC 171265), one cup. Charts that say 245 grams are rounding the same cup, so use 244 grams and you will be within a gram either way. A metric cup, 250 millilitres, holds about 258 grams, because it is a bigger cup, not because the milk is different.

Every figure is for one level US cup, 236.6 millilitres. Tablespoon figures are the cup figure divided by 16, and teaspoon figures are the tablespoon figure divided by 3.

Whole, 2 percent, and skim milk all land within a gram or two of each other, because taking fat out adds a little water and protein in its place. The two that break the pattern are evaporated milk, which has about half its water boiled off, and sweetened condensed milk, which is evaporated milk with a lot of sugar added. Sweetened condensed milk weighs about 60 grams more per cup than whole milk, so never swap them one for one.

Going the other way, 100 grams of whole milk is about 0.41 cups, 250 grams is about 1.02 cups, and 500 grams is about 2.05 cups. For tracking calories, one cup of whole milk has about 149 calories and 8 grams of protein, and one cup of skim milk has about 83 calories and 8 grams of protein, both from USDA FoodData Central.

Why milk weighs more than water

A cup measures volume and a gram measures weight, so the same cup weighs a different amount for each ingredient. Water is the baseline: one US cup of water weighs 237 grams. Milk is a little denser than water, about 1.03 grams per millilitre, because of the fat, protein, and milk sugar dissolved in it. Multiply that density by the cup volume and the maths lands on the USDA figure: 236.6 millilitres times 1.03 is about 244 grams.

That is why some charts land wrong in different directions. A chart that says 240 grams is treating milk like water. A chart that says 250 grams is using a metric cup.

US cup, metric cup, and UK cup

The cup you use changes the answer more than the milk does. A US customary cup holds 236.6 millilitres and a cup of whole milk from it weighs 244 grams. A metric cup, used in Australia, New Zealand, and Canada, holds 250 millilitres and the same milk weighs about 258 grams. A UK cup is also 250 millilitres in modern recipes, so it takes the metric figure too.

If a recipe was written in the US and you measure with a 250 millilitre cup, you add about 14 grams of milk per cup without meaning to. If a recipe was written with metric cups and you measure with a US cup, you take about 14 grams away. When a recipe gives grams, use the grams and skip the cup entirely.

Plant milks

Soy milk is the closest to dairy milk, about 243 grams a cup from USDA FoodData Central. Almond milk and oat milk sit around 240 grams a cup, but both vary by brand. Some are mostly water and some are thickened, and sweetened versions weigh a little more than unsweetened ones.

Worked example

A pancake batter calls for 1 cup of milk, 2 cups of flour, and 2 tablespoons of sugar. In grams that is 244 grams of milk, 240 grams of flour, and 25 grams of sugar. If you used a metric cup for the milk by mistake, you would pour 258 grams, 14 grams too much, and the batter would be thinner than the recipe intended.

How to measure milk

Use a liquid measuring cup, the clear jug with a spout, not a dry measuring cup. Set it on a flat surface, pour to the line, and check the level at eye height, not from above, because looking down makes the line read higher than it is.

In a Pakistani kitchen

Pakistani recipes usually mean a standard cup of doodh when they say one cup of milk, and the same 244 grams applies. The place it matters most is chai: a cup of doodh patti made with a full cup of milk carries about 149 calories from the milk alone, before sugar, while the same cup made with half milk and half water carries about half that.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of whole milk?

244 grams for a US cup, from USDA FoodData Central. A metric cup of 250 millilitres holds about 258 grams.

How many grams is 1 cup of skim milk?

About 245 grams, a gram more than whole milk, because removing the fat leaves slightly more water and protein.

How many grams is 1 tablespoon of milk?

About 15.3 grams for whole, 2 percent, skim, and buttermilk, 15.8 grams for evaporated milk, and 19.1 grams for sweetened condensed milk.

Every figure on this page is approximate, because density varies a little by brand and temperature, and by how the cup was filled. For exact work, weigh the milk.
