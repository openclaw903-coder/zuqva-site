How many grams in a cup of rice?

A US cup of dry long-grain white rice is about 185 grams, spooned in and levelled. That figure comes from USDA FoodData Central. Cooked rice is a different story: one cup weighs anywhere from about 158 to 200 grams, depending on the grain and how firmly it is packed. The trap is the word cup: a cup of dry rice and a cup of cooked rice are two different measurements, and a recipe that does not say which one can be out by a factor of three. Dry rice roughly triples in weight as it cooks, because the grains drink up water, so 185 grams dry becomes about 555 grams cooked.

Jasmine sits with long-grain white at 185 grams, though a few charts print 180 or 190. Short-grain rice is rounder and packs tighter, so its cup weighs 200 grams; sushi rice is short-grain rice under another name, and arborio, the rice for risotto, is also 200 grams a cup. Brown rice sits in between at about 190, though charts range from 185 to 195. Wild rice is a grass seed, not true rice, and its long thin grains leave more air in the cup, so it is the lightest at 160 to 170 grams.

Dry rice vs cooked rice

Cooked rice is mostly water, about two thirds of its weight, and its cup weight is a range, not one number: a cup measures volume, so how much rice fits depends on the air gaps between the grains.

The USDA lists a loosely filled cup of cooked long-grain white rice at 158 grams, and packing the same cup down brings it close to 200. Medium-grain rice is denser once cooked, at 186 grams a loose cup, and brown rice heavier still, at 202 grams. A loosely filled cup has air gaps between the grains, and pressing the rice down squeezes those out. White rice roughly triples its weight in the pot: one cup dry, 185 grams, cooks into about three cups, or roughly 555 grams. A simpler way to remember it: 100 grams dry becomes about 300 grams cooked; brown rice grows nearer two and a half times, to about 250 grams. A cup of cooked white rice carries about 205 calories, brown rice about 248, basmati about 190, jasmine about 205, and wild rice about 166. Four cups cooked needs about one and a third cups dry, about 250 grams. A tablespoon of dry rice is about 12 grams, a quarter cup about 46, and half a cup about 93.

What about a metric cup, and a rice cooker cup?

A metric cup holds 250 millilitres, not 236.6, so it holds about 6 percent more: a metric cup of dry long-grain white rice is about 195 grams, from the same density of 0.78 grams per millilitre. The rice cooker cup is a different trap. The little cup that comes with a rice cooker is a Japanese gō: it holds only 180 millilitres, and a level cooker cup of dry rice is about 140 to 150 grams, not 185. The water lines inside the cooker pot are calibrated to that cup. A cooker cup of dry white rice cooks to about 450 grams. If a recipe says one cup and you use the cooker cup, you are about a quarter short.

Worked example

Plain rice for four as a side, with a stir-fry. The recipe calls for 2 cups of dry white rice. At 185 grams a cup, that is 370 grams. It cooks to about 6 cups, around 1.1 kilograms, and at about 205 calories a cooked cup, the pot holds about 1,230 calories. A recipe that lists 500 grams of dry rice needs about 2 and three quarter cups: 500 divided by 185 is 2.7. One cup of dry rice feeds about three people as a side dish, about 60 grams dry each.

How to measure a cup of rice

Spoon dry rice into a dry measuring cup and level the top with the flat edge of a knife. Do not dip the cup into the bag: scooping packs the grains and can add 10 to 20 grams. For cooked rice, fluff it with a fork first and spoon it in loosely; pressing it down makes the cup read heavy. If a recipe gives weights in grams, weigh the rice and skip the cup.

In a Pakistani kitchen

In a Pakistani kitchen the rice is usually basmati, which weighs the same as ordinary long-grain white, 185 grams a dry cup, so any recipe written for long-grain white works gram-for-gram with basmati, biryani included.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of uncooked rice?

About 185 grams for dry long-grain white, jasmine, and basmati in a US cup. Short-grain white and sushi rice are 200 grams, brown long-grain about 190, and wild rice 160 to 170.

How much cooked rice does 1 cup dry make?

About three cups, or roughly 555 grams, for white rice. Brown rice makes a little less, about 2.5 cups.

How many grams is 1 cup of cooked rice?

It depends on the grain and the packing: about 158 to 200 grams for white rice. The USDA lists a loosely filled cup at 158 grams, medium-grain at 186, and brown at 202.
