How many grams in a cup of rice?

A US cup of dry long-grain white rice is about 185 grams. Cooked, a cup weighs about 186 grams. The two numbers look the same by accident: a dry cup roughly triples in the pot, so 185 grams dry becomes about 555 grams cooked, close to three cups. The type of rice moves the number more than the cup does. Basmati is a little lighter, short-grain a little heavier, and wild rice is much lighter.

The range is narrow for most rice. Basmati and jasmine sit with white rice at about 185 grams. The dense short grains reach 200 grams. Wild rice is the outlier at 160 grams: it is a grass seed, not true rice, with a slimmer, lighter grain.

Dry rice vs cooked rice

This is where most people get confused. A cup of dry rice and a cup of cooked rice weigh almost the same, about 185 grams, but they are not the same amount of food. The dry cup triples in the pot, while the cooked cup is mostly water.

One hundred grams of dry white rice becomes about 300 grams cooked. Brown rice grows less, about 250 grams from the same 100 grams dry, because the bran layer holds the grain together. Cooked rice is about two thirds water by weight, which is why the calories per cup stay near 200 even though the weight triples.

Why do sources say 185 or 200?

They measure differently and round differently. Spooned into the cup and levelled, long-grain white rice is 185 grams. Scooped straight from the bag and tapped down, the same cup can hold 200 grams. Fifteen grams is 8 percent, smaller than the difference between one serving and the next. The USDA lists 185 grams for long-grain white rice, and other kitchen charts round to 200 grams. Both are right for their method. It is the same story as sugar at 198 or 200 grams: how you fill the cup matters more than the last few grams.

What about a metric cup, and a rice cooker cup?

A metric cup holds 250 millilitres, not 236.6, so it holds about 6 percent more. A metric cup of dry long-grain white rice is about 196 grams. The cup that comes with a rice cooker is different again: it holds 180 millilitres, about 150 grams of dry white rice, which cooks to about 450 grams. If a recipe says one cup and you use the cooker cup, you are about a quarter short.

Worked example

A recipe serves four and calls for 2 cups of dry white rice. At 185 grams a cup, that is 370 grams. It cooks to about 6 cups, around 1.1 kilograms, and at about 205 calories a cooked cup, the pot holds about 1,230 calories. A recipe that lists 500 grams of dry rice needs about 2 and three quarter cups: 500 divided by 185 is 2.7. A tablespoon of dry rice is about 12 grams, since 16 tablespoons fill a cup. One cup of dry rice feeds about three people as a side dish, about 60 grams dry each.

How to measure a cup of rice

Spoon the rice into a dry measuring cup and level the top with the flat edge of a knife. Do not dip the cup into the bag: scooping packs the grains and can add 10 to 20 grams. Use the cup that came with the rice cooker only if the recipe says rice cooker cup. If a recipe gives weights in grams, skip the cup and weigh the rice. A kitchen scale removes the technique from the measurement.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of rice?

About 185 grams for dry long-grain white rice in a US cup. Short-grain is 200 grams, brown is 190 grams, and wild rice is 160 grams. A cup of cooked white rice is about 186 grams.

How much cooked rice does 1 cup of dry rice make?

About 3 cups, around 555 grams, for white rice. Brown rice makes a little less, about 2.5 cups.

How many calories are in a cup of cooked rice?

About 205 calories for white rice, 215 for brown, 190 for basmati, and 166 for wild rice.

Is 1 cup of rice 200 grams?

Only if you scoop it and tap it down. A level, spooned cup of long-grain white rice is 185 grams. Short-grain rice is 200 grams even when spooned in.
