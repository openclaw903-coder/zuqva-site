How many grams in an egg?

A large egg weighs about 50 grams without its shell and about 57 grams with it. The shell is thrown away, so 50 grams is the number that matters in a recipe. A cup of beaten egg weighs about 243 grams, or about 5 large eggs. If a recipe lists grams, use the weight without the shell. If it lists cups, count the eggs instead of measuring.

How much does an egg weigh by size?

Egg sizes are weight classes. The USDA sets a minimum weight for a dozen eggs in each size, so a carton labelled large holds eggs that average about 57 grams in the shell.

Large is the size most recipes mean when they say one egg. A medium egg is about 6 grams lighter, which matters in baking that depends on exact liquid, like a sponge cake. The figures are USDA grading standards.

How much of an egg is shell, white and yolk?

The shell of a large egg weighs about 6 to 7 grams, roughly a tenth of the whole egg. What is left is about 50 grams, split into white and yolk. The white is about 30 to 33 grams and the yolk about 17 to 20 grams. Sources round differently: some list 33 grams of white and 17 of yolk, others 30 and 20. For whites only, one large white is about 33 grams.

How many grams in a cup of eggs?

One US cup of beaten egg weighs about 243 grams. The figure comes from USDA FoodData Central, which lists one cup of raw whole egg at 243 grams. Eggs vary in size and beaten egg traps air, so a practical range is about 228 to 258 grams per cup. Egg whites weigh almost the same as whole beaten egg, so one cup of whites is also about 243 grams, or about 7 to 8 large whites.

How many eggs make a cup?

About 5 large eggs make one cup of beaten egg. Five large eggs weigh about 250 grams without their shells, close to the 243 grams a cup holds. Four large eggs weigh about 200 grams, which leaves the cup about a fifth short. A recipe needing 2 cups of beaten egg calls for about 486 grams, which is about 10 large eggs. Ten large eggs weigh about 500 grams, close enough that the recipe works. Count the eggs and skip the measuring cup.

Why does one egg not always weigh 50 grams?

Three things move the number. First, the size on the carton. A medium egg is 44 grams without its shell, 6 grams under a large. Second, the country. A large egg in the UK weighs 63 to 73 grams in the shell, while a US large is about 57 grams, so a British large is closer to a US extra large, and a UK medium is the closest match to a US large. Third, the egg itself: size classes set a minimum weight per dozen, so one egg can be a few grams off the average. A video going around social media says one egg is 60 grams. That number mixes the two weights: a large egg is about 57 grams with the shell and 50 grams without it, so 60 grams is neither.

The practical way to measure eggs

If a recipe gives grams, weigh the cracked egg and add or remove a spoonful of white to hit the number. If a recipe gives cups, crack 5 large eggs per cup and skip the measuring jug. If a recipe just says 2 eggs, use 2 large eggs, about 100 grams. One large egg is also about 3 tablespoons of beaten egg.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 egg?

About 50 grams without the shell for a large egg, 57 grams with it. Medium is about 44 grams without the shell, small about 38 grams.

How many eggs are in 1 cup?

About 5 large eggs, beaten, weighing about 250 grams, close to the 243 grams a US cup holds. For whites only, about 7 to 8 large whites make a cup.

Do you weigh an egg with or without the shell?

Without it. The shell weighs about 6 to 7 grams and goes in the bin, so 50 grams of egg means 50 grams of cracked egg. Weighing with the shell adds about a tenth the recipe never asked for.
