How many grams in a cup of quinoa?

One level US cup of dry quinoa weighs 170 grams and carries about 626 calories, from USDA FoodData Central. Cooked, one level cup weighs 185 grams and carries about 222 calories. The two cups weigh almost the same on the scale, yet the dry cup holds nearly three times the calories of the cooked one.

Dry and cooked quinoa by type

Every figure is for one level US cup, 236.6 millilitres. Dry figures are USDA's uncooked quinoa entry (FDC 168874), 368 calories per 100 grams, and cooked figures are USDA's cooked quinoa entry (FDC 168917), 120 calories per 100 grams.

The type changes the texture, not the weight. White quinoa cooks the softest and fluffs up the most. Red and black quinoa keep their shape and bite, so they suit salads and grain bowls. Tricolor is a mix of the three in one bag. All of them weigh the same per cup dry because the seeds are close in size and density, and the yield is the same across white, red and black.

Why one dry cup makes about three cooked cups

Quinoa drinks in water as it cooks. One cup dry weighs 170 grams and comes out of the pot weighing about 510 to 585 grams, roughly three times its dry weight. The calorie count tells the same story another way: 626 calories dry divided by 222 calories per cooked cup works out to just under three cups.

A few sources say one dry cup makes 3.5 to 4 cups. That is what you get from a wetter pot and a loose fluff, where each cup weighs less than 185 grams. The calories in the pot stay 626 either way, so the cup count and the cup weight move together. Count the cups one way and weigh them the other, and the pot still holds the same food.

Use one part quinoa to two parts water on the stove. Rinse the dry quinoa under cold water first, unless the packet says it is already rinsed, because the seeds carry a bitter coating called saponin. Bring it to a boil, simmer covered for 15 minutes, then rest it off the heat for 5 minutes and fluff it with a fork. Quinoa is done when the little white ring around each seed has opened out.

How much dry quinoa per person

As a main dish, a third of a cup of dry quinoa per person is a good serving: about 57 grams, about 209 calories, cooking into about one cup. As a side, a quarter cup dry is enough: about 43 grams, about 157 calories, cooking into about three quarters of a cup. A cup of cooked quinoa carries about 8.1 grams of protein and 5.2 grams of fibre, plus about 118 milligrams of magnesium and 2.8 milligrams of iron, all from USDA's cooked quinoa entry.

One cup of dry quinoa gives three main servings of a cup cooked, or four side servings of three quarters of a cup. Cooked quinoa keeps about 5 days in the fridge and freezes well for up to 3 months. A 1 kilogram bag holds about 5.9 cups dry, which cooks into about 17 cups, so one bag covers a lot of meals.

Worked example: quinoa for four

Quinoa for four as a main: 2 cups dry, 340 grams, about 1,252 calories. Add about 4 cups of water, simmer 15 minutes, rest 5 minutes, and the pot gives about 6 cups cooked, about 1.5 cups and 313 calories each before anything else goes in. Use red or black quinoa instead and the same 2 cups dry take 15 to 20 minutes and hold their shape better in a salad.

If a recipe calls for 1 cup of cooked quinoa, start with about a third of a cup dry; for 4 cups cooked, start with about 1.5 cups dry. Weighing is the reliable way: a cup of dry quinoa is 170 grams, and a scale removes the guesswork.

Dry versus cooked calories, and the logging trap

Dry quinoa carries 368 calories per 100 grams and cooked quinoa 120, because more than half the cooked weight is water from the pot. The calories do not change when quinoa cooks; they spread over more grams. One hundred grams dry comes out weighing about 305 grams cooked, with the same 368 calories it went in with.

Weigh cooked quinoa and log it with a dry entry and you count about three times the real calories; weigh dry and log it cooked and you count about a third. Pick the entry that matches the state you weighed it in. Work out what the portion means for your day in the TDEE calculator.

US cup, metric cup, and UK cup

A US cup holds 236.6 millilitres; a metric cup, used in Australia, New Zealand, and Canada, holds 250, about 6 percent more, so add about 6 percent to every figure here: dry quinoa goes from 170 grams to about 180, cooked from 185 to about 196. A UK cup is also 250 millilitres in modern recipes. When a recipe gives grams, use the grams and skip the cup entirely.

In a Pakistani kitchen

Quinoa is not a traditional grain in Pakistani cooking, and it turns up mostly in modern kitchens as a swap for rice: in a pulao style pot, tossed through a salad, or served beside daal. The same weights apply, and it cooks the same way, rinsed first with one part quinoa to two parts water. If a recipe says a cup of quinoa without saying dry or cooked, it usually means dry, measured before the pot.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of dry quinoa?

170 grams, about 626 calories, from USDA FoodData Central (FDC 168874).

How many grams is 1 cup of cooked quinoa?

185 grams, about 222 calories, with about 8.1 grams of protein and 5.2 grams of fibre, from USDA's cooked quinoa entry (FDC 168917).

How many cups cooked does 1 cup of dry quinoa make?

About 3 cups. The calorie count works out to just under 3 cups, and wetter cooking with loose fluffing can stretch it toward 3.5 cups.

How many grams is a tablespoon of dry quinoa?

About 11 grams, about 39 calories. A tablespoon of cooked quinoa is about 12 grams and 14 calories.
