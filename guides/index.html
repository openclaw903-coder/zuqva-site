Kitchen conversions

  • Why grams and millilitres are not the same thing in cooking — Why 100 g of flour is not 100 ml, and the density formula that converts them.
  • Common kitchen conversions, with worked examples — Common kitchen conversions worked out in full, with the method shown every time.
  • How many grams in a cup of flour? — One US cup of all-purpose flour is 120 to 125 g, depending on how you fill the cup.
  • How many grams in a cup of sugar? — One US cup of granulated sugar is 198 to 200 g — about 75 g more than a cup of flour.
  • How many grams in a cup of rice? — One cup of dry long-grain rice is about 185 g; cooked, a cup weighs 158 to 200 g.
  • How many grams in a cup of oats? — One cup of dry rolled oats is about 81 g; steel-cut oats weigh far more per cup.
  • How many grams in an egg? — A large egg is about 50 g without its shell; a cup of beaten egg is about 243 g.
  • How many grams in a tablespoon? — One tablespoon is not 15 g for most ingredients: flour 7.5 g, sugar 12.5 g, honey 21 g.
  • How many grams in a cup of milk? — One US cup of whole milk weighs 244 g; skim, evaporated and condensed differ.
  • How many grams in a cup of pasta? — One US cup of dry pasta weighs 64 to 122 g by shape; cooked, a cup is 105 to 124 g.
  • How many grams in a cup of cocoa powder? — One US cup of unsweetened cocoa powder is 86 g; brands run 75 to 95 g a cup.
  • How many grams in a cup of lentils? — One cup of dry lentils is 192 g; cooked, a cup is 198 g, and one dry cup makes 2.5 to 3 cooked cups.
  • How many grams in a cup of quinoa? — One cup of dry quinoa is 170 g; cooked, a cup is 185 g, and one dry cup makes about 3 cooked cups.
  • How many grams in a cup of heavy cream? — One US cup of liquid heavy cream is 238 g; whipped, a cup is 120 g, and the King Arthur gap is explained.
  • How many grams in a cup of shredded cheese? — One cup of shredded cheddar is 113 g; bagged mozzarella is only 86 g a cup, fresh is 112 g.
  • How many grams in one roti? — A standard roti comes from about 30 g of atta; calories by size, phulka to large.
  • Pakistani kitchen measurements: atta, ghee, and desi recipes in grams and cups — Desi measures in grams and cups: atta, suji, ghee, besan, katori and chammach.
