How many grams in a tablespoon?

There is no single number. A tablespoon measures volume, and a gram measures weight, so the same spoon holds a different weight for each ingredient. One level US tablespoon of flour is about 7.5 grams, of sugar about 12.5 grams, of butter about 14 grams, and of honey about 21 grams. The 15 gram figure you see in many charts is only true for water. Flour weighs about half of that, and honey weighs about 40 percent more.

Every figure is for one level US tablespoon, spooned in and levelled, not heaped. Cup weights come from the King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart, and each spoon figure is the cup weight divided by 16, since 16 tablespoons fill a US cup.

Why 15 grams is wrong for most ingredients

One US tablespoon holds 14.79 millilitres, which cooks round to 15. A tablespoon of water weighs 14.8 grams, so 15 grams is right for water and close enough for milk. It falls apart for everything else. Flour is mostly air between fine particles, so a spoon of it weighs about half the water figure, while honey is a dense syrup at 21 grams.

Flour is also the figure charts argue about: King Arthur's spooned-and-levelled cup gives 7.5 grams, density tables about 7.8, and OmniCalculator 8.9. The spread comes from how the flour was packed, not from a different spoon.

So 3 tablespoons of flour are 22.5 grams, while 3 tablespoons of sugar are 37.5 grams. Using 15 grams a spoon for both would double the flour and add a fifth too much sugar.

Why salt needs its own warning

With salt, the type matters as much as the spoon. Fine table salt is 18 grams a tablespoon, or 6 grams a teaspoon. Diamond Crystal kosher salt is only about 8 grams a tablespoon, because its hollow flakes trap air, while Morton kosher salt sits in the middle at about 16 grams. A recipe written for kosher salt will taste far too salty if you measure table salt by the spoon.

Level, rounded, and heaped spoons

Every figure so far is for a level spoon. A heaped spoon holds much more, and the gap depends on the ingredient.

A rounded spoonful of peanut butter is about 22 grams and 130 calories. A spoon straight from the jar is almost always heaped.

US, metric, and Australian tablespoons

A US tablespoon is 14.79 millilitres and a metric tablespoon, used in the UK, Canada, and Europe, is 15 millilitres, a difference of about 1 percent, so the gram figures on this page work for both. Australia is the exception: its tablespoon is 20 millilitres, four teaspoons instead of three, and about 35 percent bigger than the American spoon. An Australian cup holds 250 millilitres, so it holds 12 and a half Australian tablespoons. A metric cup of flour is about 127 grams instead of 120, from the same 250 millilitres against 236.6 for a US cup.

Worked example

A pancake batter calls for 3 tablespoons of flour, 2 tablespoons of sugar, 1 tablespoon of butter, and 1 teaspoon of salt. In grams that is 22.5 grams of flour, 25 grams of sugar, 14.2 grams of butter, and 6 grams of salt, about 68 grams in all. If the recipe had said 4 tablespoons of honey instead of sugar, that would be 84 grams against about 50 grams of sugar.

How to measure a level tablespoon

Use a proper measuring spoon, since eating spoons vary in size. Spoon the ingredient in and level the top with a knife; do not dip the spoon into the bag for flour, since scooping packs it down and can add a fifth or more.

In a Pakistani kitchen

Pakistani recipes often measure in tablespoons, and the same figures apply: a tablespoon of atta is close to whole wheat flour, about 7 grams. The one thing to watch is salt: Pakistani recipes usually mean fine iodised table salt, which is the 18 gram tablespoon, not kosher salt.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 tablespoon of flour?

About 7.5 grams, spooned in and levelled, from a cup weight of 120 grams. A heaped tablespoon is about 12 grams, so level the spoon.

How many grams is 1 tablespoon of sugar?

About 12.5 grams, from a cup weight of 198 grams. Packed brown sugar is a little heavier, at about 13.3 grams a tablespoon.

How many grams is 1 teaspoon?

A teaspoon is a third of a tablespoon: flour 2.5 grams, sugar 4.1 grams, butter 4.7 grams, honey 7 grams, table salt 6 grams.

Every figure on this page is approximate, because density varies by brand and packing, and by how the spoon was filled. For exact work, weigh the ingredient.
