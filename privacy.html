Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect nothing from you. There is no account, no signup, no tracking pixel on this site, and the calculators do all their maths in your own browser. When you close the tab, your numbers are gone.

What is not collected

  • Your age, weight, height or activity level — typed into the TDEE calculator, used once, never sent anywhere.
  • Your recipe amounts — typed into the kitchen converter, used once, never sent anywhere.
  • Your name, email or location — unless you choose to write to us through the contact page, in which case only what you write is involved.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If a future version ever adds advertising, this page will be updated first, and the change will be stated here in plain language.

External links

Guides may link to published sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.
